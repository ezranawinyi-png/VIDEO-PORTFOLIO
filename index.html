Quality assurance

AI video evaluation

A good evaluator looks beyond whether a clip looks attractive. The key question is whether it follows the brief and maintains visual and temporal consistency.

Evaluation framework

What I assess

  • Prompt alignment
    Does the output match the requested subject, action, setting and style?
  • Temporal consistency
    Do objects, people and scenes remain coherent across frames?
  • Motion quality
    Does movement appear smooth, logical and continuous?
  • Visual quality
    Are composition, lighting, detail and clarity effective?
  • Technical quality
    Are there visible artefacts, glitches or audio issues?
Illustrative evaluation reportExample format
